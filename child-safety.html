Child Safety Standards

Let's Hangout with Friends · Last updated: November 2026

Let's Hangout with Friends (the "App") has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These are the standards we publish and follow.

1. Who can use the App

The App is for adults only. You must be 18 years or older to create an account. If we find that a person under 18 has an account, we remove it.

2. What is not allowed

  • Any content that sexualises, exploits or endangers children, including images, videos, text, voice, usernames, bios and room names.
  • Grooming, or any attempt to contact a child for sexual or abusive purposes.
  • Sharing, requesting, trading or advertising CSAM, or links to it.
  • Nudity or sexual content of any kind.

These rules are written in our in-app Terms & Conditions and apply to posts, reels, videos, comments, chat messages, calls, voice rooms and profiles.

3. How to report a concern inside the App

Users can report content or people directly inside the App:

  • Posts, reels and videos: 3-dot menu → Report.
  • Chat messages: long-press a message → Report.
  • Users: from their profile or the chat menu → Report user.
  • Users can also Block anyone at any time.

4. What we do when we receive a report

  • Reported content may be hidden automatically while it is reviewed.
  • We review reports and remove content that breaks these standards.
  • We suspend or permanently delete accounts that break these standards, and may block the phone used.
  • Where the law requires, we report CSAE and CSAM to the relevant regional and national authorities, such as the police and child protection bodies.

5. Compliance

We comply with the child safety laws that apply to us, and we follow Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy.

6. Contact

For child safety concerns, or to report CSAM or CSAE outside the App, write to our designated point of contact:

letshangout.support@gmail.com

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services or police first.
