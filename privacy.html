Privacy Policy

Let's Hangout with Friends · Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy explains what information Let's Hangout with Friends collects, why, and the choices you have. We do not sell your personal data.

1. Information you give us

Account details (e-mail, username, password), profile photo, bio and optional details such as school, college, job, city, hometown, relationship status and interests, posts, reels, videos, comments, likes, chat messages, friend requests, room activity, gifts and reports.

2. Information collected automatically

Device and app information (device model, Android version, app version, language), push-notification token, basic usage and crash information, and security logs such as login time. We do not collect your precise location.

3. Permissions we ask for

Camera (photos, reels, video calls). Microphone (voice rooms, calls, video recording). Photos/videos (choose from gallery). Notifications (messages, calls, friend requests). Each permission is used only for that feature and you can turn it off in your phone settings.

4. How we use information

To run the App, show your content to the people you choose, connect calls and rooms, send notifications, keep the App safe (spam, abuse, copyright reports), fix bugs and improve features, and follow the law.

5. Who can see what

Public accounts can be seen by other users; private accounts only by friends. Your personal details (About) are shown only to people who can see your profile. Chat messages and calls are visible only to the people in them. Blocked users cannot see you.

6. Services we use

We use trusted providers to run the App: Supabase (database, login and file storage), Agora (live voice and video), Google Firebase (push notifications) and Google Play (purchases, when available). They process data only to provide their service and have their own privacy policies.

7. Storage and security

Data is stored on secure cloud servers and sent over encrypted connections. Access to data is limited by account rules. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password and do not share it.

8. How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is active. When you delete content it is removed from the App. When your account is deleted we remove or anonymise your data, except where we must keep some records for safety or by law.

9. Your choices and rights

You can edit your profile and details, make your account private, delete posts, block and report people, and turn off permissions or notifications. You can ask to see, correct or delete your data by writing to letshangout.support@gmail.com.

10. Children

The App is not meant for children under 13. If we learn that a child under 13 has an account we will delete it. Parents can contact us to ask for removal.

11. Changes

We may update this policy and will tell you in the App about important changes.

12. Contact

Questions or requests: letshangout.support@gmail.com.
