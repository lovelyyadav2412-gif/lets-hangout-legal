Delete your account

Let's Hangout with Friends

In the app

Open the app → Profile → Edit profile → scroll to the bottom → Delete account and confirm.

By e-mail

If you cannot open the app, write to letshangout.support@gmail.com from the e-mail address of your account with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete your account and data.

What is deleted

Your profile, posts, reels, videos, comments, messages and settings are removed or anonymised. We may keep limited records where required for safety or by law (see the Privacy Policy).
